Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Fourtop is built so your tip numbers stay yours. Your shifts live on your phone and in your own iCloud, and I never see them. Here's exactly what that means.

What Fourtop stores

Fourtop keeps the following on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework:

  • Your shifts: date, hours, hourly wage, cash tips, card tips, sales, and tip-out
  • Your jobs and tip-out rules
  • Your tax setup answers (filing status and a rough band for other income)
  • Your settings

None of it is sent to me or to any server of mine.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, Fourtop can use Apple's CloudKit to keep your data in step across your own Apple devices. It lives in your private database, in your iCloud account. I can't see it, and nobody else can either. Apple's privacy policy covers how iCloud handles it.

To turn it off, go to iOS Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Apps Using iCloud, and switch Fourtop off.

What Fourtop doesn't do

  • No accounts, logins or sign-ups
  • No analytics
  • No ads or ad networks
  • No tracking, and no data sold or shared, because I don't have any to share
  • No server of mine that the app talks to

In-app purchase

Fourtop Pro is a one-time purchase of US$9.99 (product com.charithp.fourtop.pro). Apple's App Store handles the payment through StoreKit. I never see your card, your name or your Apple ID. Pro is tied to your Apple ID, so you can restore it on any device you sign in to with the same one.

Tax figures are estimates

Fourtop's tax figures are estimates for planning, not tax advice. They cover federal income tax only. State income tax and FICA aren't counted. For anything that matters, check with a tax professional.

Children

Fourtop doesn't collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it, and bigger changes will be in the App Store release notes.
