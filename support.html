How do I restore my purchase?

If you bought Pro before, on this phone or another, you don't pay again.

  1. Open Fourtop and tap the gear at the top of Home.
  2. Tap Restore purchase.
  3. Wait a moment while it checks with the App Store.

Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. If it says it found nothing, check you're signed in to that one in iOS Settings, then try again.

What's free, and what's Pro?

Free has no limit on shifts. You get tip-out as a dollar amount, take-home and real hourly on every shift, and your last 30 days of history.

Pro is US$9.99, once, with no subscription. It adds:

  • The no-tax-on-tips tracker, with the $25,000 cap and a tax-saved estimate
  • Tax setup
  • Jobs and tip-out rules
  • Full history and a year view
  • A year-end W-2 check, and CSV and PDF export
  • iCloud sync

How is real hourly worked out?

Your wage times your hours, plus your tips after tip-out, divided by your hours. Take-home is cash tips plus card tips, minus what you tipped out.

Are the tax numbers advice?

No. They're estimates for planning, not tax advice. They cover federal income tax only. State income tax and FICA aren't counted. Ask a tax professional about your own return.

Can I use Fourtop on more than one device?

Yes, with Pro. Sign in to the same iCloud on each device and your shifts follow you. There's no Fourtop account to make. Your data stays in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.

Where do my shifts live?

On your phone, and in your own private iCloud if sync is on. I don't have a server, so I can't see or recover them. Deleting the app removes the copy on that phone.

Refunds

Apple handles every purchase, so refunds come from them too. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. I can't issue one myself.

Is there an Android version?

Not for now. Fourtop is iPhone only.
